### SuppS1_bin_size_comparison

**Shows.** Two panels that record the pre-registered comparison deciding the project's spatial bin width. Left: per-animal split-half tuning reliability (Pearson *r*, y-axis; each point is the median over that animal's units) at 2.5 cm (orange) and 5 cm (blue) bins, one paired pair of points per animal joined by a grey line, grouped along the x-axis by area (DMS, DLS, ACC, V1, CA1, DG). Right: the sub-5 cm structure test, d_str = r_fine − r_cross, plotted as one jittered green point per animal-area with a black median bar and a dashed zero line; values above zero would favour the finer grid. Every point in the left panel moved upwards from orange to blue — 5 cm was more reliable in 50 of 50 animal-area cells — and every area median in the right panel sat below zero, so no sub-5 cm structure was detectable.

**Methods.** Input was the task cohort's two spatial products, `preprocessed_data2p5cm.mat` and `preprocessed_data5cm.mat`, both emitted by a single dual-bin run of `ProcessStriatumTask.m` over the same 16 mice and identical trials (paired-file identity enforced by asserts on animal count, unit count, trial count and every area mask, `compare_bin_sizes.m:51,63-76`). Bin geometry replicated the pipeline exactly — corridor_end 200 a.u., 1 a.u. = 1.25 cm, bin widths 2 a.u. (2.5 cm, 100 bins) and 4 a.u. (5 cm, 50 bins), last edge widened (`compare_bin_sizes.m:31-36,177-181`). Firing rates were in Hz with NaN marking unvisited bins. Per unit, tuning curves were trial-means over disjoint odd and even trial sets, and three Pearson correlations were computed over jointly finite bins: r_fine (odd vs even at 2.5 cm), r_coarse (odd vs even at 5 cm), and r_cross (odd-trial fine curve vs the even-trial coarse curve linearly interpolated onto the fine grid with extrapolation) — `bin_comparison_metrics.m:36-63`. Zero fractions were taken over visited entries only, with non-finite rates recoded as unvisited (`bin_comparison_metrics.m:67-72`). Areas with fewer than five units in an animal were skipped (min_units = 5, `compare_bin_sizes.m:55`); the comparison therefore ran on 392 DMS units in 15 animals, 520 DLS in 10, 699 ACC in 15, 264 V1 in 5, 133 CA1 in 3 and 17 DG in 2 — 50 animal-area rows drawn from 16 distinct mice (`figures/compare_bin_sizes_summary.csv`). **Caveats.**
- The decisive metric is asymmetric by construction. The interpolated coarse predictor carries less sampling noise than the fine one, so d_str ≤ 0 is the expected direction even when moderate sub-5 cm structure exists. The panel licenses "no sub-5 cm structure detectable at this trial count", not "none exists" — the code states this in its own docstring (`bin_comparison_metrics.m:14-20`) and prints the warning at run time (`compare_bin_sizes.m:128-133`).
- Both arms inherit the unfixed occupancy-denominator defect: spatial firing rate is spikes / dwell-time with the per-bin duration computed as `(bin_times(end) − bin_times(1))/1000`, i.e. (k−1)·dt rather than k·dt (`spatial_binning.m:45`), giving speed-dependent rate inflation. The comparison is internally paired, so the contrast is fair, but the absolute reliability values are not clean.
- Split halves were odd versus even trials across the whole session, pooling naive, intermediate and expert trials. Reliability is therefore not epoch-specific, and units that remap across learning are penalised in both arms.
- Ten uncorrected tests were run — two (d_rel and d_str) in each of the five areas that passed the n ≥ 3 guard. No multiple-comparison correction was applied. At n = 5 (V1) the exact signed-rank floor is p = 0.0625 and at n = 3 (CA1) it is p = 0.25, so neither area could reach significance; DG (n = 2) is plotted but was excluded from the statistics by the n < 3 guard, so its −0.116 median carries no p-value.
- The 50/50 tally is not 50 independent observations: the same 16 mice contribute to several areas, so animal-area cells are correlated across areas.
- The panel is a historical one-off and cannot currently be regenerated. No 2.5 cm product exists anywhere in the repository (`processed_data/` holds `preprocessed_data5cm.mat`, `preprocessed_data_control5cm.mat` and `preprocessed_data_control2.mat`), so reproduction requires temporarily setting `bin_size = 2` in `ProcessStriatumTask.m`. The figure is dated 2026-08-10 15:43 and predates the current preprocessing cache (2026-08-11 23:37) and the 2026-08-12 cell-type fix; cell type does not enter this analysis, but the numbers have not been re-derived on the current cache.

### SuppS2_TCA_balance_comparison

**Shows.** Four bar panels, one per matched rank-4 TCA component, testing whether the per-area unit-balancing subsample drives the decomposition. Each panel plots the mean neuron-factor loading per area (DMS, DLS, ACC, V1, CA1), normalised to the largest area, for the balanced tensor (blue, 228 units per area) against the unbalanced full tensor (orange). **Methods.** Both tensors came from `Run_TCA_pipeline.m` on the combined task-and-control supermouse tensor built from 14 task and 5 control animals, [units × 50 spatial bins (5 cm) × 30 LP-aligned trials]; 255 units (9.4%) carrying any NaN were dropped, leaving 2465 (`processed_data/tca_rerun_2026-08-11.log:50-54`). ### SuppS3_CCA_contrasts

**Shows.** Four matched-cell scatter plots of held-out first canonical correlation (CC1), each point one (animal, pair, epoch) cell coloured by striatal-triangle pair, with the identity line drawn and axes fixed to [−0.15, 0.75]. The panels contrast, in turn, plain against partial CCA at 25 ms; plain against partial at 10 ms; FS-included against FS-excluded at 25 ms partial; and 10 ms against 25 ms binning. Each carries the median difference and a Wilcoxon p on per-animal medians. The result is a set of method-sensitivity effects and one sign reversal against prediction: with fast-spiking units excluded, partialling out the other recorded areas *raises* CC1 rather than lowering it, so the triangle's coupling is pair-specific rather than shared drive — though this reversal is confined to the FS-excluded frame, and with FS units included plain and partial are indistinguishable. Including fast-spiking units changes nothing in the partial frame; finer temporal binning uniformly lowers CC1 in all four configurations.

**Methods.** Numbers come from `Striatum project/tcca/results/grid_summary.csv`, computed by `tcca/scripts/analyze_epoch_grid.py:163-183` and plotted by `tcca/scripts/figs_epoch_grid.py:154-201`. The underlying grid is 8 configurations — temporal bin {25, 10 ms} × FS {excluded, included} × CCA {partial, plain} — each run by `tcca/scripts/run_epochs.py` over task learners only. Per learner and area pair, a lagged subspace readout was fitted on running, in-corridor bins within each epoch: 1 ms spikes were Gaussian-smoothed (σ = 2.5 ms) and summed into 25 or 10 ms bins; the running gate was 2.0 cm/s; unit activity was per-trial-mean-subtracted (residual CCA) and z-scored per unit; each area was reduced by PCA to K = 20 (set in the driver at `run_epochs.py:32`, overriding the config's default sample-based k rule), recorded per cell as k_eff = min(20, n_units_x, n_units_y), with at least 5 units per area and at least 600 running bins per cell (`run_epochs.py:33-34`, MIN_CELL_BINS = K × 30); cross-validation was 5-fold over whole trials (seed 0); significance used a per-trial circular-shift surrogate with 100 shuffles and a minimum shift of 15 bins (seed 0) — 375 ms at 25 ms bins, 150 ms at 10 ms; cells with CC1 ≥ 0.99 were dropped as saturated (`run_epochs.py:121`). In the partial configurations the control Z was every *other* area present for that animal, concatenated (`src/striatum_tcca/runner.py:66-67`), its dimensionality recorded per cell as n_units_z; `--no-partial` gives plain CCA. Epochs followed the project learning-point rule (z ≤ −2, window 10, ≥7 consecutive), 10 trials per epoch, with animal 8 forced to non-learner. Contrasts were taken over matched (animal, pair, epoch) keys restricted to the DMS-DLS / DMS-ACC / DLS-ACC triangle; the fraction of positive cells and the median difference are cell-level descriptives, while the p-value is a two-sided Wilcoxon signed-rank (scipy, `method="auto"`, exact at these n) on per-animal median differences, keeping animals as the inferential unit. Values: plain minus partial, FS-excluded, was −0.012 at 25 ms (32.4% of 68 cells positive, p = 0.016, n = 9 animals) and −0.031 at 10 ms (34.8% of 69 cells, p = 0.039, n = 9), but in the FS-included frame it was null — −0.0006 at 25 ms (42.5% of 80 cells, p = 0.164, n = 11) and −0.0014 at 10 ms (40.7% of 81 cells, p = 0.426, n = 11). FS-included minus FS-excluded in the partial frame was +0.004 at 25 ms (54.4% of 68 cells, p = 0.426, n = 9) and +0.003 at 10 ms (53.6% of 69 cells, p = 0.109, n = 9), but in the plain frame +0.016 at 25 ms (65.2%, p = 0.039, n = 9) and +0.010 at 10 ms (78.3%, p = 0.0039, n = 9). The 10 ms minus 25 ms difference was −0.034 to −0.040 in all four configurations (p = 0.0020 to 0.020, 20-31% of cells positive). For context, the same grid's learning tests were null throughout: across 8 configurations × 3 pairs = 24 naive-to-expert strength tests, exactly one was nominally significant (b25/FS-included/plain, DLS-ACC, p = 0.039) and none survived BH within its configuration; the information-flow index was likewise flat, with 0 of 420 (configuration × pair × integration-window) cells showing a naive-to-expert change either nominally or after BH, out to ±250 ms (`tcca/results/grid_ifi_windows.csv`; 4 configurations × 3 pairs × 10 windows at 25 ms plus 4 × 3 × 25 at 10 ms = 420).

**Caveats.**
- No multiple-comparison correction is applied across the 12 contrast rows. BH is used only within the three strength tests of a configuration and within the IFI window sweep, never across this contrast family; **eight of the twelve** contrasts are nominally p < 0.05 (both FS-excluded plain-vs-partial rows, both plain-frame FS rows, and all four bin-size rows).
- Power is thin and the p-values sit on a coarse discrete grid. Contrasts rest on 9 to 11 animals and 68 to 81 cells; at n = 9 the exact two-sided signed-rank p-values are multiples of 2/512 ≈ 0.0039, so the observed 0.016 and 0.039 are only the 4th and 10th attainable values above the floor.
- The reported n_animals is the count of finite per-animal deltas; the signed-rank test strips zero deltas before the scipy call (`src/striatum_tcca/paired_stats.py:29-31`), so the n entering the test can be smaller than the n printed on the panel.
- Animals contribute unequal numbers of cells, a known coverage imbalance in the tcca outputs. Taking per-animal medians before the test absorbs this for the inference but not for the cell-level descriptives (median difference, fraction positive).
- The two arms of each contrast are the same spikes analysed twice, so these are method effects rather than independent measurements; no effect size beyond the median difference is reported.
- The bin-size contrast confounds counting noise with lag resolution: 10 ms bins have fewer spikes per bin as well as a finer lag grid, so the CC1 drop is expected on signal-to-noise grounds alone and must not be read as a timescale result. The circular-shift minimum is specified in bins, so its physical floor also differs between the two arms (375 vs 150 ms).
- Only one of the four FS contrasts is plotted (25 ms, partial); the 10 ms partial and both plain-frame FS contrasts exist in `grid_summary.csv` but not in the figure, and it is the plain-frame ones that are significant. Likewise only the FS-excluded plain-vs-partial contrasts are plotted, and those are the two that show the effect; the FS-included versions are null.
- Reading "partialling is denoising" requires that the partialled-out areas carry shared drive rather than part of the pair's own signal. Z is every other area present in that animal, so the control dimensionality is large and the partial regression could absorb genuine coupling. That Z composition also varies between animals, which is not modelled.
- The IFI is flat for *learning change* (0/420), but not identically zero: 5 of 420 cells had a BH-surviving non-zero pooled IFI, consistent with the registered note of a small DLS-ACC asymmetry visible only in the least-controlled configuration.
- No LP-shuffled null exists anywhere in the project, so the epoch structure underlying every cell in these scatters is never tested against a null in which the learning point is randomised.
- Negative CC1 values are genuine cross-validated failures, not clipping artefacts; the axis deliberately starts at −0.15 (`figs_epoch_grid.py:161`).

Spatial (`cca`): min_units 6; 250 shuffles; circular shift with a ≥8-bin minimum (40 cm at 5 cm bins); k from the samples rule, k = floor(n_samples / 15) capped at 30, not a fixed 20 (`cca/src/striatum_cca/config.py:118-167`).

**`Fig4E_network_schematic` — illustration, but constrained by what the results support.** What the artefacts license is a static, pair-specific triangle, not a learning-dependent network: per-animal held-out CC1 is significantly above zero for all three striatal pairs in the spatial arm (worst p = 0.016) with no epoch effect (animal-level rm-ANOVA p = 0.854 DMS-DLS, 0.450 DMS-ACC, 0.904 DLS-ACC); the temporal arm shows no learning change in any of 24 naive-to-expert strength tests after correction (one nominal hit, zero BH); the information-flow index is flat at every integration window to ±250 ms (0/420 learning-change cells, nominally as well as after BH); and coupling is *reduced* when the other recorded areas are left in — though only with fast-spiking units excluded — indicating pair-specific rather than shared drive. Any arrow in the diagram implying a learning-related change in connectivity, or a direction of flow, would not be supported by any artefact currently in the repository.
